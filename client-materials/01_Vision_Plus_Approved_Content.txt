# 02 — ABOUT VISION PLUS

## Built on Experience. Driven by What’s Next.

VISION PLUS was established in **Qatar in 2017** with a clear purpose: to make advanced technology practical, connected, and dependable.

From the beginning, our work has been centered on understanding how technology performs in real environments — not simply how it appears on a specification sheet.

As technology evolved, so did we.

In **2021, VISION PLUS expanded into Egypt**, bringing our experience, engineering approach, and systems integration capabilities into a new market while continuing to develop our expertise across security, connectivity, automation, and intelligent technologies.

Today, VISION PLUS delivers integrated solutions across **Mobile NVR & Mobile Surveillance, CCTV & Security, Access Control, Networking & ICT, ELV, Audio Visual, Smart Building & Home Automation, and Fire Alarm Systems**.

What defines us is not how many technologies we provide, but how effectively we make them work together.

Our involvement extends across the complete technology lifecycle — from consultancy and system design to project management, installation, integration, testing, commissioning, training, maintenance, and long-term technical support.

Whether technology is protecting a building, connecting a workplace, automating a home, or monitoring a fleet moving across a city, our objective remains the same:

### Make technology work intelligently, reliably, and as one.
